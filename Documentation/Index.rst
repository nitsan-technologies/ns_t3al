===============
AI Localization
===============

:Extension key:
   ns_t3al

:Package name:
   nitsan/ns-t3al

:Version:
   1.0.0

:Language:
   en

:Author:
   `T3Planet <https://t3planet.de>`__ & TYPO3 contributors

:License:
   GPL-2.0-or-later

:Rendered:
   |today|

----

**AI Localization** translates the language files (XLIFF,
``Resources/Private/Language/*.xlf``) of your TYPO3 extensions right in the
TYPO3 backend. Edit the English source and several target languages side by
side in a spreadsheet-like grid, let DeepL, OpenAI, Gemini or Google Translate
fill the empty translations, review them and save the files. Translations of
TYPO3 system extensions can also be uploaded to Crowdin.

.. figure:: Images/translate-result.png
   :alt: AI Localization module with the label grid after DeepL filled the German translations
   :class: with-border with-shadow
   :zoom: lightbox

   The AI Localization module after an AI translation run.
