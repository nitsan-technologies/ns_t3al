=======
Support
=======

Need help with **AI Localization** (``EXT:ns_t3al``)? The
T3Planet team helps with installation, configuration, provider setup,
Crowdin and licensing questions.

*  `T3Planet Support <https://t3planet.de/support>`__ – technical support
   and customer assistance
*  `Product page <https://t3planet.de/t3al-typo3-erweiterung>`__

When you contact support, please include your TYPO3 version, the extension
version, the provider you used and the exact message shown in the module.
